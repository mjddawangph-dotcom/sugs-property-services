## 4. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary: High Desert Homeowner
- **Age:** 35–65
- **Location:** Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley (primary); Rancho Cucamonga (secondary)
- **Income:** $70k–$120k household
- **Home type:** Single-family, owner-occupied
- **Pain points:** No time for yard work; HOA compliance; yard looks neglected; previous landscaper was unreliable
- **Buying trigger:** Spring season, HOA notice, selling home, aging/health preventing DIY
- **Decision maker:** Homeowner (often both partners)

### Secondary: Property Manager / Small Commercial
- **Type:** HOA boards, rental property owners (1–10 units), small commercial properties
- **Pain points:** Need reliable recurring vendor; tenant complaints; turnover cleanup
- **Value:** Higher LTV, less price sensitivity, recurring contracts

## 5. Unique Selling Points (USPs)

## 6. Competitive Positioning

**Market observation (preliminary):** The High Desert landscaping market is fragmented. Established players exist (Sedillo & Son, Golden Ace, ADI) but most are either older/lower-digital-presence or focus on design/build (Monarch). There's a gap for a **clean, well-reviewed, digitally-present maintenance company** that shows up on time and communicates professionally.

**Positioning statement:**
> For High Desert homeowners and property managers who are tired of unreliable landscapers, Sug's Property Services delivers on-time, detail-oriented lawn care and yard restoration at a fair price—backed by years of professional crew leadership.

**Competitive research:** Deferred to SEO strategy phase (on hold until GBP approved). Preliminary names: Sedillo & Son, Golden Ace, ADI Landscaping, Monarch (design/build, different segment).

## 7. Pricing Philosophy

- **Recurring maintenance:** Monthly contract (weekly or biweekly visits). Price per visit depends on lot size and scope.
- **One-time services:** Quote-based after site assessment.
- **Weed treatment:** Per-visit or bundled with maintenance.
- **Clean ups:** Quote-based (highly variable scope).
- **No published pricing on landing page initially** — "Get a Quote" CTA.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about pricing, service areas, and how the work actually runs.

How do your prices work?

Lawn mowing starts at $50 per cut, property cleanup at $150, and irrigation repair at $85. Those are starting points for qualifying routine work. The final price depends on the size of the property, its condition, and access. Landscaping, sod, and weed work are quoted per property.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Send the address and a few details, or call, and we will look at the property and give you a price before any work starts.

Which areas do you serve?

Victorville, Hesperia, and Apple Valley routinely. Other parts of San Bernardino and Riverside County depend on the ZIP and the current schedule. Send the address and we will confirm rather than guess.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes. We work daily, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, including Saturday and Sunday.

How soon can you come out?

It depends on the season and the current schedule. Call and we will tell you honestly what the next available window looks like instead of promising a date we cannot hold.

Do you do one-time visits, or only schedules?

Both. A one-time mow, cleanup, or repair is fine. Most customers eventually move to a schedule because it keeps the property from sliding back to where it started.

What is included in a mowing visit?

Mowing at a consistent height, edging along walks and drives, string trimming around fences and beds, and blowing off hard surfaces. Shrub trimming, fertilization, and seasonal cleanup can be added.

Do you haul away the debris?

Yes, haul-away is part of the scope we quote for cleanups. We look at the volume before pricing so there are no surprises on the day.

Do you install artificial turf?

No. We install natural sod only. We would rather tell you now than quote a job we do not do well.

Do you work with rentals and property managers?

Yes. We work with homeowners and managed properties. Tell us about the property and who the point of contact is, and we will work out a scope that fits.

Still not sure what you need?

Most people call knowing the yard is a problem but not knowing which service fixes it. That is fine. Describe what is going on — weeds through the gravel, a dry patch, a lawn that has gotten away — and we will tell you what the work actually is and what it costs.

If it turns out to be outside what we do, we will say so instead of quoting it anyway.

Yard and property cleanup in Victorville

For yards that have gotten away — overgrowth, weeds, debris, and years of buildup, brought back to something you can actually maintain.

When a cleanup is the right call

A cleanup is for a property that has gotten away: weeds through the gravel, shrubs grown into the walkway, debris that has been sitting since the last tenant, or a yard that simply got skipped for a season. It is also the right first step before sod, rock, or planting, because new work does not go in over mess.

Typical situations we quote for:

  • Moving in, or getting a rental ready for a new tenant
  • Preparing a property to sell or for an inspection
  • A yard that went unattended through the summer
  • Before a sod install or landscape reset
  • Buildup around fences, beds, and side yards

What is included

  • Cutback of overgrown grass, weeds, and shrubs
  • Weed removal from beds, gravel, and hard-to-reach edges
  • Debris and green waste collected and hauled off
  • Hard surface cleanup along walks, drives, and patios
  • Disposal of what we remove, within the quoted scope

What is not included

We do not do tree removal, stump grinding, demolition, hauling of hazardous material, or major concrete work. If a cleanup uncovers something outside that scope, we stop and talk to you about it before doing anything else.

What happens after the cleanup

A cleaned-up property stays that way only if something keeps up with it. Most customers either move onto a regular mowing schedule or book a seasonal cleanup once or twice a year. We will tell you which one actually makes sense for your property rather than selling you the bigger package.

Questions

Cleanup questions

What does a cleanup actually cover?

Cutting back overgrowth, removing weeds, clearing debris and green waste, and cleaning up the hard surfaces. The point is to get the property back to a state that can be maintained, not just to make it look better for a week.

Do you haul everything away?

Haul-away is part of the scope we quote. Some properties produce a little, some produce a trailer load. We look before we price it so there are no surprises on the day.

Can you do a one-time cleanup without a maintenance plan?

Yes. Plenty of customers just want the reset. We will tell you honestly how long it will hold without follow-up maintenance, and then it is your call.

Do you take on hoarder or heavily neglected properties?

Sometimes. It depends on what is in the debris and whether there is safe access. Send photos and describe the situation and we will tell you straight whether we can take it on.

How much does a cleanup cost?

Cleanups start at $150. The final price depends on how much growth and debris there is, how far material has to be carried, and disposal. We quote from the property, not from the phone.

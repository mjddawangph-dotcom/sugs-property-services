Lawn mowing in Victorville, CA

Scheduled mowing, edging, and trimming for homes and managed properties in Victorville, Hesperia, and Apple Valley.

What a mowing visit includes

A cut is not just a cut. Every visit covers the things that make a lawn look maintained rather than recently hacked at:

  • Mowing at a consistent height for the season, not a scalp
  • Edging along walks, drives, and patios
  • String trimming around fences, beds, posts, and walls
  • Blowing off hard surfaces so clippings do not end up in the house

We keep the height steady from visit to visit. Cutting too low in the heat is one of the fastest ways to turn a green lawn brown out here, and it is usually what happens when a lawn gets skipped for a few weeks and then cut back hard to catch up.

Weekly, every other week, or one-time

Most customers choose a set schedule. Through summer, weekly visits keep the lawn from getting ahead of the mower. In the cooler months, every other week is usually enough. One-time mowing is available for a property that just needs to be brought back to an even height before a cleanup or a sale.

A cleanup and a mowing schedule work well together. If the yard has gotten away from you, start with a property cleanup, then move onto a routine.

Fertilization and trimming

Fertilization and shrub and tree trimming can be added to a visit rather than booked separately. Trimming on a regular cycle keeps plants from getting woody and leggy, and it is easier on the plant than cutting it back hard once a year.

Questions

Lawn mowing questions

How often should a lawn be mowed here?

Through the hot months, most lawns in the High Desert need mowing about once a week. In cooler weather growth slows and every other week is often enough. If you are not sure, a first visit tells us a lot about how fast your grass is growing.

Do I have to be home?

No. Many customers are not home during service. We just need to know how to get into the yard and where the gate is, and we will confirm the details when you book.

Can I book a single mow instead of a schedule?

Yes. One-time mowing is available, though scheduled visits are usually better value because the lawn never gets far enough ahead to cost extra time.

What happens if it rains on my service day?

Wet grass tears instead of cutting cleanly, so we may move the visit. We will let you know rather than leave ruts in the yard.

Do you trim shrubs and trees too?

Yes, shrub and tree trimming is available on the same visit, along with fertilization and seasonal cleanup. Ask for it when you request the quote so it goes into the scope.

Sod and turf installation in Victorville

A lawn that establishes instead of drying out in the first week comes down to prep — grading, soil, and irrigation that actually covers the area.

Prep is the whole job

Sod that fails almost never fails because the sod was bad. It fails because the ground underneath it was not ready: uneven grade that pools water, compacted soil with no root zone, or a sprinkler system that does not actually cover the area.

So the prep is the part we take seriously:

  • Clear old material, weeds, and debris
  • Grade for drainage, so water moves instead of sitting
  • Improve the soil where it is too thin or too compacted
  • Check irrigation coverage before anything is laid
  • Lay and roll the sod tight, with no gaps or overlaps

Natural sod, not artificial turf

We install natural sod. We do not install artificial turf, and we will say that up front rather than quote a job we would not do well. If artificial turf is what you want, there are companies that specialize in it.

The first two weeks decide it

New sod needs consistent moisture until it roots — more water than an established lawn, applied more often, then tapered down. Too little and it shrinks and dies at the seams. Too much and it rots or lifts. We give you a watering schedule at handoff, and it is worth following closely for the first couple of weeks.

If you also need the rest of the yard addressed — beds, rock, weeds, irrigation — a landscaping reset handles it in one project instead of three.

Questions

Sod installation questions

Do you install artificial turf?

No. We install natural sod only. If you want artificial turf, we are not the right company for that job and we will tell you so rather than take the work.

How soon can I walk on new sod?

Light foot traffic is usually fine within the first couple of days, but heavy use should wait until the sod has rooted. We give you the specifics for your install at handoff.

What if my irrigation does not cover the new lawn?

That is the most common reason new sod fails. We check coverage before installing. If the system needs repair or adjustment first, we will tell you before the sod goes down rather than after it dies.

Can you install sod over an existing lawn?

Sometimes, but not always. It depends on what is there and what the soil underneath is like. We assess it on site and explain what we recommend.

How much does sod installation cost?

It is quoted per property by square footage, prep required, and whether irrigation work is needed. Send photos and the approximate area and we will give you a range to start from.

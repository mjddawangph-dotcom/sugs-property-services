Landscaping overview

How pricing works

The starting prices above are for qualifying routine work. The final number depends on the size of the property, its current condition, and access — a quarter-acre of clean lawn and a quarter-acre of knee-high weeds are not the same job.

We confirm scope and price with you before any work begins, and we do not add charges afterward without talking to you first. If the property turns out to need more than the quote covered, you will hear about it before we proceed.

What is included in a maintenance visit

  • Mowing, edging along hard surfaces, and string trimming
  • Blowing off walks, drives, and patios
  • Shrub and tree trimming on request
  • Fertilization on request
  • Seasonal cleanup on request

We do not do pest control, tree removal, or major concrete and hardscape construction. When a job is outside what we do well, we say so.

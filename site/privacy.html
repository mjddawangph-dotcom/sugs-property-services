Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Sug's Property Services respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

Information you provide

When you submit the quote form, we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, email address, property address, requested service, and any project details you choose to include.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, discuss the property, prepare a quote, arrange scheduling, and follow up about the work. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Form processing

Quote requests are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service, and delivered to our business email. Formspree may store submissions as part of providing that service. Its handling of your information is governed by Formspree's own privacy policy.

Service-area checker

The service-area checker compares the ZIP code you enter with our current service list in your browser. The address or ZIP you enter is not saved by the checker. It is only a preliminary screening tool; final availability is confirmed directly with you.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses local browser storage to remember your light or dark theme choice and session storage to remember that you dismissed the service-area checker. Those values stay in your browser.

The site serves its font files directly and may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider. The hosting provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details as part of delivering the page.

How long we keep information

We keep quote and contact information only as long as needed to respond to the request, provide or discuss services, and maintain ordinary business records. We delete information when it is no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of the information you submitted. California residents may have additional rights under applicable privacy law. We do not sell personal information.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when it was last revised.
